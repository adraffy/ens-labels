Resolver Keccak Brute-force LabelStore labels.json (49.3MB)
Examples:
  • Enter a hash or click an example to find its label.
  • A labelhash is the keccak256 of the label encoded as UTF-8: f(label) = hash → label ∈ f-1(hash)
  • The token ID of a .eth ERC-721 is its labelhash. ENSv2 token IDs replace the low 32 bits with a version, and also match.
  • Labels are collected from ENS contract events, primary names, and the ENSv2 LabelStore. Unknown hashes can be checked on-chain.
  • labels.json is only downloaded when you search.
